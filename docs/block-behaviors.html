Block Behaviors

28 custom block behaviors under the polyfills: namespace. Click any card for full documentation.

Machine & Renderer

polyfills:renderer

PolyFormula-driven particles, displays, sounds on any CE block without a machine.

polyfills:data_machine

Fully data-driven single-block machine. Reads from machines/*.json.

polyfills:data_multiblock

Multiblock-assembled data machine with paged storage.

polyfills:data_motor

Data-driven rotational motor. Generates RPM/SU from fuel or gas. Reads motors/*.json.

Pipes & Fluids

polyfills:pipe_block

Fluid pipe segment. Auto-connects to adjacent pipes/pumps/tanks.

polyfills:gas_pump

Extracts gas from provider blocks below, pushes into steel pipe network.

polyfills:machine_pump

Fuel-driven fluid pump. Extracts from source blocks, pushes into copper pipes.

polyfills:valve_block

Redstone-controlled fluid valve - opens/closes flow.

polyfills:fluid_tank_block

Multi-block fluid storage with fill gauge.

polyfills:gas_provider

Natural gas source (e.g. nitrocalcite). Gas pumps extract from these.

Physics & Mechanics

polyfills:fan_block

Air fan - propels entities and enables processing (blast, wash, smelt).

polyfills:spike_block

Damages entities on contact.

polyfills:magnet_block

Attracts/repels item entities within a radius.

polyfills:spreading_block

Spreads to adjacent blocks over time (like mycelium).

polyfills:bubble_block

Upward bubble column + slow-falling effect.

Crops & Growth

polyfills:bush_block

Harvestable plant that regrows over time.

polyfills:vertical_crop_block

Grows upward (like sugarcane) with configurable max height.

polyfills:tearing_crop_block

Crop with growth stages, harvested at max age.

polyfills:change_over_time_block

Transforms into another block after N ticks (drying, curing, oxidizing).

Storage & Logic

polyfills:storage_block

Custom inventory block (barrel/chest variant).

polyfills:custom_crafter

Shapeless crafting block with configurable recipe.

polyfills:redstone_operator

Boolean logic on redstone inputs (AND/OR/NOT/XOR).

polyfills:redstone_controller

Emits configurable redstone signal, togglable by click.

polyfills:chainery_block

Chain conveyor system - item routing and speed control.
